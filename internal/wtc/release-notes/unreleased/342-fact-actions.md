## OpenClaw

- Fit repository rows to their occupied columns and align genuinely narrow
  folded facts with their bulk controls. Click `±N` to request local commits,
  or the column's `±` for eligible repositories together. Hover, pending and
  brief observed-success feedback retain the same cell dimensions. Publication
  remains separate. See [fact actions](docs/openclaw-fact-actions.md) (#342).

- Gateway base catch-up preserves staged, unstaged and untracked edits in a
  pinned recovery copy and restores staging after the merge. Failed restoration
  remains explicit and blocks blind retries. Group Catch up previews its target
  rows on hover/focus. Pending or unconfirmed commit cells open action details,
  with a diagnostic agent-help handoff that does not retry the operation.

- Ship local-edit recovery in the OpenClaw catch-up skill. A verified finish
  operation archives only its exact active marker and retains backups; matching
  native completion receipts settle pending recovery feedback without a retry,
  commit or publication.
