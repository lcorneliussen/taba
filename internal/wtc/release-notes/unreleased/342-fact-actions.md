## OpenClaw

- Fit repository rows to their occupied columns and align genuinely narrow
  folded facts with their bulk controls. Click `±N` to request local commits,
  or the column's `±` for eligible repositories together. Hover, pending and
  brief observed-success feedback retain the same cell dimensions. Publication
  remains separate. See [fact actions](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/blob/541bd5b5cce3c776b2a5a3142caa869571077a1b/docs/openclaw-fact-actions.md)
  ([#342](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/issues/342)).

- Gateway base catch-up preserves staged, unstaged and untracked edits in a
  pinned recovery copy and restores staging after the merge. Failed restoration
  remains explicit and blocks blind retries. Group Catch up previews its target
  rows on hover/focus. Pending or unconfirmed commit cells open action details,
  with a diagnostic agent-help handoff that does not retry the operation.

- Ship local-edit recovery in the OpenClaw catch-up skill. A verified finish
  operation archives only its exact active marker and retains backups; matching
  native completion receipts settle pending recovery feedback without a retry,
  commit or publication.

- Catch-up now refuses incoming tracked paths that would replace ignored local
  data, on clean checkouts as well as dirty ones. Unfinished operations remain
  refused. Sandbox delivery and worker merges share the recovery lock and refuse
  unverified recovery before changing HEAD or the index. Commit controls honor
  the current action plan and recheck recovery before requesting commits.
  Partial commit progress retains its unresolved request reservation, and push
  preparation/confirmation recheck that reservation before dispatching.

- Share repository-action reservations across default and chosen steps, native
  catch-up and worker controls. Explicit acknowledgement releases the original
  request in every view; delayed responses cannot revive it or release a newer
  request. Missing handoff responses stay unconfirmed rather than enabling a
  duplicate mutation.

- Recovery verifies staged and unstaged marker text against their corresponding
  saved layers, preserving legitimate partially staged fixtures while refusing
  newly introduced marker lines.

- Recovery and sandbox continuation share marker verification that decodes
  Git-quoted paths and separates patch headers from added content. Unverifiable
  diffs stop before recovery completion or a sandbox merge commit.
