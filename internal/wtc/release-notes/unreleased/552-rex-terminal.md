## Collections

- **Open collections in Rex.** With `WTC_MULTIPLEXER=rex`, or in a Rex
  terminal on a machine without herdr, `taba open`, `taba new --open` and
  `taba retire` keep each collection in its own Rex session, and
  `taba catch-up` and `taba status --procs` find it there; herdr stays the
  default. See `taba docs instructions/rex.md`.
  ([#552](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/issues/552)).
