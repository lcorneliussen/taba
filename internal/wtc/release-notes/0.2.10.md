# taba/wtc 0.2.10

A small follow-up release. Interrupting `review run` stops its reviewers,
ordinary Bitbucket reads keep moving behind queued confirmations, and the status
archive lists the latest merge or closure first. Each change is a bounded part
of its issue; the issues stay open for the rest.

**Upgrade notes.** No configuration or pin changes are needed beyond installing
the release. A public review that leaves a changed result as a link or special
file now fails with `is not a regular file; results were not returned` instead
of reading through it. A reviewer's descendant that leaves its process group is
still not stopped; its later writes are kept out of results, but this is not a
sandbox. Bitbucket decline times remain unknown.

## Review

- **Interrupting `review run` stops its reviewers.** Ctrl-C, `SIGTERM` or `SIGHUP`
  now stops every running reviewer with its process group before the command
  exits, removes a public run's temporary directory, and leaves no summary or
  verdict to post. A late write from a process that left its reviewer's group
  cannot become a result of that run or a later one
  ([#487](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/issues/487), [#520](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/pull/520)).

## Status

- **Keep ordinary Bitbucket reads moving behind confirmations.** A confirmation
  queue at or above the request burst no longer blocks lists, checks, builds and
  PR details indefinitely while waiters keep asking; limits and cooldowns are
  unchanged. Keyed reads take turns in order; unkeyed page reads share one turn,
  and older clients can still delay them. Deferred PRs never observed show
  unknown checks with the actual refusal reason and retry time
  ([#424](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/issues/424), [#521](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/pull/521)).
- **Archived PRs list the latest merge or closure first.** The archive sorts by
  each PR's merge or provider-reported closure time across repositories, with
  undated Bitbucket declines after dated rows in a stable order
  ([#511](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/issues/511), [#522](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/taba/pull/522)).
